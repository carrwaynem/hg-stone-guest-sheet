Wayne M. Carr, Hidden Gems Financial

About Wayne

Wayne Carr is a Financial Architect and the founder of Hidden Gems Financial in the Tampa area. He has worked in financial services since 2004 and in insurance since 2015. He holds a Bachelor of Science with a focus in finance and economics, and he hosts the Mining Gems podcast.

He designs cash flow, banking, and protection strategies. He starts with goals, not products.

Goal for this interview

  • Speak to families, business owners, and entrepreneurs.
  • Help them see where their money goes each month, and why cash flow comes before any product.
  • Show that a conversation, not a pitch, is how Hidden Gems works.
  • Point listeners to a free discovery call and the free Cash Flow Accelerator guide at hiddengemsfinancial.com.

Seven topics to cover

Each topic has a question you can ask and the main point Wayne will make. Free flowing is fine. Jump in anywhere.

1Where does the extra money go?

AskAfter the bills are paid, where does the extra money go each month?

Most owners can name their sales but not where each dollar lands. Wayne explains why mapping cash flow comes first.

2Income is not a wealth strategy

AskWhy can a business owner earn a lot and still feel stuck?

Earning and keeping are different jobs. Every dollar needs a destination.

3Your own banking system

AskWhat does it mean for a business owner to be their own bank?

How the Infinite Banking Concept gives access to cash without waiting on a lender. Wayne shares an example from a contractor who pays crews weekly and repays when jobs close. No client names.

4What your CPA does not plan

AskWhere does a financial architect fit next to the CPA and the attorney?

CPAs file what happened. Wayne plans what should not happen, and works alongside the advisors an owner already has.

5Myths about Infinite Banking

AskWhat do critics get right, what do they exaggerate, and what is flat wrong?

An honest look at the criticism, including who this strategy does not fit.

6A conversation, not a pitch

AskWhat happens on a first call with Wayne?

He listens first, designs second, and will say so when a strategy does not fit. He pulls up beside you, not across the table.

7Reaching people the way they take in information

AskPeople read, listen, watch, and skim. How do you reach all of them without doing four times the work?

Branding and productivity as a way to reach as many people as possible. One conversation becomes clips, a post, and a newsletter, so each person can take it in their own way. Wayne uses Loop to do this in one pass, and Stone shares what he has learned about repurposing and transforming interviews.

Good to know

  • Wayne speaks about strategies in general terms. He does not give personal advice or promise results on air.
  • Client stories are anonymous.
  • Wayne is glad to talk through the Loop demo before the interview.
